Standing rule The theme is always the real scenes

One theme, and it is photographic. There is no size exception: a 40px trust badge is the same theme as a 1600px hero, so an illustrated or anime face is out even where it is small enough to read as decoration. That retires the two illustrated trust icons (student-boy-thumbsup, student-girl-heart), the flat 2D Cluby set in public/assets/characters/, and the drawn blog covers. The Book A-0 cover being illustrated does not license an illustrated style in the product — it is source material for the characters, not a style. Re, 5 Oct 2026. Canon: src/config/klovers-world.ts → visualSystem.standingRule, which wins if the two disagree.

Rules Colour & icon rules

All three brights — yellow, green and red — force charcoal on top: white is unreadable on every one of them. Red is the weakest of the three at 3.7:1, which is why it never carries small text.
