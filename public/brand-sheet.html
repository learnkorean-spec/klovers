04 Colour & icon rules

Both brights are so light that nothing but charcoal can sit on them. That one fact explains most of these rules.

+10 XP
Do

Green as a fill, charcoal on top.

+10 XP
Don't

Green text on ivory or white — it disappears (1.3:1).

Done
Don't

Yellow and green touching. They blur into one colour; put charcoal between them.

Level up!
Do

Yellow on charcoal for badges and the dark “royal” moments.

Do

Icons always on a tile, 80% of the tile, same corner radius in a row.

Don't

Recolour, grey out or shrink painted icons below 32px. For “locked”, use a charcoal tile + lock chip.
